Profile

Two toolkits, one way of thinking. Engineering taught me how systems behave. Consulting taught me how to explain why they matter.

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Queen's University. On the technical side I've worked hands-on with circuits, embedded systems and lab builds: designing, testing, breaking and fixing things until they perform.

Alongside that I've built consulting experience: scoping client problems, digging into the data, and turning analysis into clear recommendations. Running both tracks means I can go deep on the technical detail and still step back to see the business case.

Outside the lab you'll find me reading earnings reports, following the stock market, or talking about anything with an engine (or a battery pack).
